# Open Inventory Helper for Steam Privacy Policy

_Effective October 10, 2026._

Open Inventory Helper for Steam has no server, ads or analytics. The developer receives no user data.

## What data is processed and where it goes

The extension works on steamcommunity.com and store.steampowered.com. Requests go directly from your browser to the services below, only to provide the extension's features:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| steamcommunity.com | Price requests and the actions you start yourself (selling, buying, trades), made with your open Steam session | Community Market prices and the actions you trigger |
| api.augmentedsteam.com | Ids of the games, DLC and packages on the open store page, the store country code, the currency code | Historical lows and exchange rates |
| api.isthereanydeal.com (only if you saved a key) | Your IsThereAnyDeal key, the game id, the store country code | Price history chart |
| Skinport, Market.CSGO (TM), Waxpeer (only if you enabled the source) | The game id and the currency | Marketplace price lists |

The extension never reads or sends your Steam password, Steam Guard codes or session cookies.

## What is stored

Settings, the IsThereAnyDeal key and cached prices are stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage`). The extension keeps no browsing history. You can clear the cache in the settings (Prices → Saved prices); everything else is removed together with the extension.

## Sharing with third parties

The extension does not sell or share data. The services listed above are subject to their own privacy policies.
